# Algorithmic Trading Research

This repository documents my ongoing research into the design, testing and evaluation of algorithmic trading systems.

My objective is not to present a finished or guaranteed profitable trading system, but to document a structured research process: from strategy formulation to historical testing, forward testing, failure analysis and iterative improvement.

## Research Process

My current methodology follows this general cycle:

**Market Idea → Trading Rules → Backtest → Forward Test → Audit → Improvement → Retest**

A strategy is not considered robust simply because it performs well in historical testing.

The purpose of forward testing is to observe how the system behaves under conditions that were not part of its development process.
